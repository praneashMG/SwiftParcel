Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out — we're happy to help.

How do I track my parcel in real time?

Simply log into your customer dashboard and paste your tracking number into the "Track Parcel" field. You'll see the live GPS location, ETA, and full scan history instantly.

What areas do you deliver to?

We cover 180+ cities and 12,000+ pincodes nationwide, plus shipping to 18 international countries. Check our Coverage page to see if your pincode is supported.

How much does shipping cost?

Rates start from $6.00 for local same-day, $8.50 for domestic standard, and $18.50 for express air. Use our Rate Calculator for an instant, exact quote.

Is my parcel insured during transit?

Yes — every shipment is covered up to $5,000 by default. You can increase coverage to $10,000+ for a small add-on fee during booking.

How do I book a doorstep pickup?

Log into your dashboard, click "Book Pickup", enter the address and time slot, and confirm. A courier will arrive within your chosen window.

Can I cancel or reschedule a shipment?

Absolutely. You can cancel or reschedule any pickup free of charge before the courier arrives — just manage it from your dashboard.
