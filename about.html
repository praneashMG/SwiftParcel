Our Heritage & Vision

From a Corner Boutique to a Global Supply Hub

What began in 2012 as an artisanal stationery boutique dedicated to fine fountain pens and fountain-safe paper has evolved into an omnichannel supply power-house serving over 120,000 corporate desks, design studios, and institutions.

Our Core Pillars

What Sets Us Apart

Six uncompromising principles that guide our product selection, vendor partnerships, and customer support.

Precision & Ergonomics

Every pen, rollerball, mechanical pencil, and cutting tool undergoes rigorous balance and flow testing to eliminate user fatigue during work.

Sustainable Paper Sourcing

We partner exclusively with FSC-certified paper mills, prioritizing chlorine-free stock, plant-based non-toxic inks, and fully recyclable corrugated transport packaging.

Organized Simplicity

From color-coded lever arch files to modular desktop sorters, our filing solutions are engineered to make document organization rapid and intuitive.

Wholesale Transparency

Clear tiered pricing with zero hidden fees, automated volume discounts, free evaluation sample kits, and convenient 30-day corporate payment terms.

Rapid Desk Delivery

Running out of essential paper or notebooks shouldn't halt workdays. We maintain strategic hubs for reliable same-day and 48-hour delivery nationwide.

Dedicated Supply Specialists

Every corporate client is paired with a direct account manager who understands your exact brand specifications, paper GSM, and restock schedules.
