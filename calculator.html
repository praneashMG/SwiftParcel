How We Price

What Goes Into Your Shipping Cost

Four transparent factors determine your final price — no mystery, no fine print.

Parcel Weight

Heavier parcels cost more to move. We charge per 0.5 kg after the first kg.

Distance

Local, domestic, or international — the further it travels, the higher the rate.

Delivery Speed

Same-day and overnight options cost more due to priority handling.

Insurance & Extras

Additional coverage, signature, and alerts add small optional fees.
